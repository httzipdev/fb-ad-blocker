Privacy policy

Your data stays yours

FB Ad Blocker does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension stores

Two values, saved only in Chrome's local extension storage on your device:

  • Whether blocking is switched on or off
  • How many ads have been blocked (a number)

What it does not do

  • It does not read, record or send your posts, messages, friends, browsing history or any other content.
  • It does not use analytics, cookies, trackers or remote code.
  • It does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.

Permissions

  • storage: to save the on/off setting and the counter described above.
  • www.facebook.com: to find sponsored posts on Facebook pages and hide them. The extension runs on no other site.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is FB Ad Blocker free?

Yes. It's free, with no account, subscription or in-app purchases.

Which Facebook languages are supported?

FB Ad Blocker recognizes the sponsored label in English ("Sponsored") and Vietnamese ("Được tài trợ"). If Facebook is set to another language, sponsored posts may not be detected yet.

An ad slipped through. What should I do?

Facebook changes its layout often. Open the extension popup and click "Report issue", or use the report form, so we can update detection.

Does it work on the Facebook mobile app?

No. It's a Chrome extension for desktop, and it runs on www.facebook.com.

I turned blocking off. Why are ads still hidden?

Posts that were already removed stay hidden until you reload the page. New ads will show while blocking is off.

Is this an official Facebook or Meta product?

No. FB Ad Blocker is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc.
